# Terms-of-service self-check

**English** | [中文](tos-review.zh-CN.md)

This page is a maintainer **self-check record, not legal advice**. It records, for each product, the terms version, measurement-relevant terms, and a comparison with the collection method actually used by this project. If you find a mismatch, contact the maintainers through the private channels in [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md); the takedown mechanism is described on that page.

## Common collection method

For every product, a maintainer uses the official interface or client manually and in the ordinary way, sends a minimal input, and records usage that the product itself displays or writes to disk. The project does not automate consumer accounts, intercept or decrypt network traffic, reverse-engineer or modify clients, or bypass rate or quota limits. Screenshots are published only after opaque redaction. Every published measurement includes complete reproduction information: protocol, product version, configuration, and native units.
